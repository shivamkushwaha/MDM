Optimal allocation

Model notes

All supply is shipped and all demand is satisfied. Costs are minimized globally.

What is calculated?

  • Transportation: exact minimum cost feasible shipment plan.
  • Transshipment: minimum cost flow satisfying node balances and arc capacities.
  • Assignment: minimum cost one to one matching.

The engine computes the optimal result directly. It does not animate VAM, MODI or Hungarian textbook steps in this version. When a problem has more than one optimal solution, the allocation shown may differ from a hand solution with the same total cost.

Classroom use

  1. Change one cost or constraint.
  2. Observe the new allocation and objective value.
  3. Discuss why routes or assignments change.
  4. Restore sample to start again.
Runs locally in your browser. Uses a successive shortest path minimum cost flow solver.
