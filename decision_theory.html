3. Decision Making Under Uncertainty

These criteria ignore probabilities. The starred row is the recommended alternative.

Laplace (equal likelihood)

Treat every chance event as equally likely and take the simple average of each row.

Hurwicz (criterion of realism)

Blend the best and the worst payoff of each alternative using the coefficient of optimism alpha. Hurwicz value = alpha × best + (1 − alpha) × worst

Pessimist (0) Optimist (1) alpha = 0.50

Minimax Regret (Savage)

Regret is the gap between a payoff and the best payoff available under the same chance event. Choose the alternative whose maximum regret is smallest.

4. Decision Making Under Risk

Weighted Payoff: Expected Monetary Value (EMV)

Each payoff is weighted by the probability of its chance event. EMV = sum of (probability × payoff)

Expected Opportunity Loss (EOL)

The same weighting applied to the regret table. The alternative with the smallest EOL is always the one with the best EMV.

5. Perfect Information and Its Value

With perfect information the decision maker learns which chance event will occur before choosing, and so always picks the best alternative for that event.
