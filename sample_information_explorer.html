2. The Information Source and the Decision Tree

The two likelihoods describe the track record of the source: how often it gave a positive report in past cases of each kind. A perfect source would have 1 and 0. A source with equal likelihoods tells us nothing.

Squares are decisions and circles are chance events. The number beside each node is its expected value. Thick coloured branches are optimal; dashed branches are rejected. The fee is deducted at the root.
